Biography

Purity M. Omariba is a results-oriented Communications Specialist with over five years of professional experience managing internal and external communications, media relations, public advocacy campaigns, digital communications, and multimedia content production in the non-governmental and development sectors.

Her expertise lies at the intersection of strategic communication and social advocacy. Purity specializes in translating complex program goals into tailored communication frameworks that amplify organizational visibility, strengthen stakeholder trust, and drive policy dialogue. She has consistently demonstrated an ability to turn ground-level field achievements and participant feedback into compelling narratives across television, radio, digital platforms, and print media.

Throughout her work with multi-grant projects and cross-functional teams, Purity incorporates gender-sensitive and gender-transformative approaches into all strategic messaging. By leveraging multimedia tools—including photography, documentary planning, podcasts, and digital campaigns—she ensures that human-centered storytelling remains the focal point of community outreach and international resource mobilization initiatives.
